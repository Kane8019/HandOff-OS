# Proposal

> Fictional, illustrative demo. Parties and facts are invented. Not legal advice.

## Goal

Finalize a fictional plain-language agreement letter between **Alice Example**
and **Bob Example** so it could (in the demo) be "sent" to the other party.

## Decision needed

Owner approval to treat the draft as final and "send" it (in this demo, that
means marking it ready and recording the approval).

## Scope

- One fictional agreement letter with placeholder parties and terms.
- Plain language, neutral tone, a placeholder signature block.

## Out of scope

- Any real legal matter, party, court, case number, or jurisdiction.
- Legal strategy or legal advice of any kind.
- Reuse of this content for a real document.

## Reversibility

Low once "sent." Sending a legal-style document to another party is hard to walk
back, which is exactly why this is high-consequence and gated behind review and
owner approval. As a draft, it is fully reversible.

## Risks

- Sending before review → wrong or unclear terms reaching the other party.
- Treating the fictional content as real → mitigated by clear "fictional"
  labeling throughout.

## Requested approval

Owner approval, after a completed review, to mark the fictional letter final.

## Execution notes

Do not "send" before: (1) the review verdict is PASS, and (2) the owner records
explicit approval. Then log the approval in `history-log.md`. The human owner is
the only approver.
